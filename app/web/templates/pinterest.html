How this works
  • Available products: Only successful Fourthwall listings (READY) appear, across all workflows. A completed job alone is not enough; jobs without a successful listing, including skipped or failed listing attempts, do not appear.
  • Check visibility: A successful listing may still be hidden in Fourthwall. Confirm the product is publicly published before promoting it; this page does not check live storefront visibility.
  • Schedule a pin: Connect or reauthorize Pinterest and ensure the product link is known. Missing links are looked up automatically; use Retry lookup or Edit link if needed. Select Schedule pin, then review the board, image, copy, and future time. Copy and image choices are prefilled from the job where available.
  • Keep the app running: Scheduling happens here, not on Pinterest. Overdue pins are picked up after restart; active workflow work and the rolling daily limit can delay publishing.
  • Repeat pins: Only one scheduled or publishing pin is allowed per listing. After publication, a {{ cooldown_days }}-day cooldown applies. Adjust Pinterest limits in General Settings.
  • Review attempts: Check the Pins section for status, cancel scheduled pins, or retry failed attempts. If the publication outcome is unknown, check the Pinterest board before confirming a retry to avoid duplicates.
