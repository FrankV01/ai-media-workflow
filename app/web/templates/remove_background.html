Background Remover

Drop PNG files to cut out a solid-color background and get a transparent PNG back. Pure color-keying — no AI model. Best on flat backdrops; gradients are not supported. Max 25 MB per file, up to 20 files.

Removal settings

Samples the image corners and uses the median color as the key. Uncheck to pick the color yourself.

Key color override

How strictly colors must match the key (0–100). Raise it if background specks remain; lower it if the subject starts eroding. 10–30 suits most art.

Fill strategy

Softens the cutout edge. 1–2 px removes hard jaggies on anti-aliased art.

px

Shrinks the kept area inward by N pixels — use 1–3 px to strip colored fringe left along the edge.

Removes background color bleed on edge pixels (e.g. green tint on a green-screen cutout). Best combined with a little feather.

Quick guide — Contiguous vs Global

  • Contiguous (default): only removes background touching the image edges. Same-color details inside the subject — white eyes, highlights, holes — stay intact.
  • Global: removes every matching pixel anywhere. Use when the subject's interior colors never collide with the backdrop, or when enclosed pockets of background must also go.
